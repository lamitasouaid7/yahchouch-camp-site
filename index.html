QUESTIONS
What time are check-in and check-out?

Wooden tents: check-in 5:00–10:00 PM (latest 10:00 PM), check-out 4:00 PM the next day. Village Houses and Cave: check-in 2:00 PM, check-out 12:00 PM the next day.

Can I bring my own food?

Yes, but not into the pool area — outside food and drinks aren’t allowed there unless bought from our restaurant, which is open until 11:00 PM. There is no restaurant near the Village Houses, so bring food for your stay.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome at the campsite, but not on the hiking trail, which runs through a protected nature reserve.

Can children use the pool?

No. The pool is for guests aged 18 and over only.

Can I bring a speaker?

No speakers, please — we play music throughout the camp.

What should I bring for sleeping?

The wooden tents have a bed with a mattress only, so bring your own bed cover, blanket and pillows. Towels are provided in the Cave; in the Village Houses bring your own towels, and any extra guests bring sleeping bags for the couches.

How many people fit?

The wooden tents fit 3 (+$10 for the third person). The Village House for 2 is set up for two and fits up to four; the Village House for 4 is set up for four and fits up to six; the Cave fits up to four. Each extra guest in the houses and the Cave is +$10 per night.
